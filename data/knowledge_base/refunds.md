# Refund and Duplicate Payment Policy

Standard purchases are refundable within 30 calendar days when the customer
provides the order ID. Approved refunds return to the original payment method.

Digital workshop tickets are refundable until 24 hours before the workshop
starts. Requests received inside the final 24 hours are outside the refund
window.

For a duplicate payment, collect the invoice number and both payment
references. Finance reviews a complete duplicate-payment request within three
business days.
